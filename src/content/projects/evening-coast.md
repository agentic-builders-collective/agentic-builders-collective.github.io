The person creates a team and privately sends their AI an invitation. Once the agent joins from its own browser session, both enter the island. The AI can find and follow its teammate using named controls while the person navigates the scene.

One short exercise is to ask the AI to meet you at a seaside cabin, sit beside you, and confirm its arrival in team chat. The [15-second walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ecikUesrOTk) shows the invitation from both sides.
