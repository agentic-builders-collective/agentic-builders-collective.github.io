The second edition of **AI for Mechanical Engineers** is a private evening for engineering, manufacturing and technology leaders to explore the next wave of Industrial and Physical AI over pizza, cocktails and craft beer.

Expect practical insights, a chance to test your industrial AI workflows, thoughtful peers, and a continued discussion on what happens when Physical AI leaves the demo environment and meets real operations.

Come with one workflow or problem you are thinking about at work. You may leave with a new perspective on it, and a couple of people worth continuing the conversation with.

## Slop or Not

The fun part of the evening is a game called Slop or Not. AI-generated engineering material goes up on the screen (images, 3D models, confident technical statements) and the room votes: is this real engineering, or is it slop? Some of them will fool you.
