Explore how a solo founder can run a business with QwenWork agents. This hackathon has two tracks: automating business operations, and building governance, security and decision-making safeguards for AI agents. Cash prizes of up to S$8,000 are available.

The **17 November kickoff workshop** includes food, networking and idea development. Bring a laptop. Participants from any background can join, including those without developer experience; approved participants receive a month of QwenWork access.

- **23 November:** submit a three-minute video demo.
- **30 November:** the top 10 finalists are contacted by email.
- **11 December:** finalists pitch live, followed by the prize presentation.

[Register on Luma](https://luma.com/kxld1xck). Host approval is required, and the exact Singapore address is available after registration.
