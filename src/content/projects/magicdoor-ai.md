Magicdoor.ai is a practical front door to multiple AI models. Instead of subscribing separately to every provider, users can compare and use leading chat and image models from one account, with transparent pay-as-you-go usage and no subscription required.

The product focuses on reducing AI subscription fatigue: choose the model that fits the job, switch when a provider hits limits, generate and edit images, and keep usage costs visible as you work.
