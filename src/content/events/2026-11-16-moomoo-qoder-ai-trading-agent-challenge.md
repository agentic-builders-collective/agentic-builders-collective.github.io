Build a trading agent with Qoder Cloud Agents and test it through Moomoo MCP using simulated funds. The challenge covers market research, strategy execution and risk management, with over S$8,500 in cash, trading vouchers and Qoder credits available as prizes.

The kickoff workshop on **16 November** includes demos, trading strategy discussions and networking. Doors open at **6:00 PM**, with presentations starting at **7:00 PM**. Bring a laptop.

- **17–28 November:** development and paper-trading window.
- **30 November, 11:59 PM SGT:** submit a Qoder-generated presentation and runnable agent package.
- **11 December:** top 10 finalists shortlisted.

Approved participants receive a month of Qoder Pro, 1,500 bonus credits and a paper-trading account starting with US$100,000 in simulated funds.
