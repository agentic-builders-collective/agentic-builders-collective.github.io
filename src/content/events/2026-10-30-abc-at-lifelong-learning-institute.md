ABC meetup #11 brought new faces and familiar builders together for an evening of practical demos and sharing. Jensen Loke demonstrated his team's Devin workflow and running Qwen 3.8 Max through ModelScope in Claude Code. Bennett Clement shared his custom software factory, Chris Pecaut discussed his book journey, YJ Soon demonstrated Amp, cloud agents and the outer loop, and Brian Chew shared how he uses Codex and Grokbot.

Thanks to SG Code Campus for sponsoring the food and venue, and to Jules Ng and the team for supporting the evening. Photos by Brian Chew.
