Stop staring at charts. Let AI agents do the heavy lifting and win over S$8,500. Moomoo and Qoder are calling algo traders, AI builders and developers to turn trading ideas into working AI agents.

Build agents on the Qoder Cloud Agents platform to research the market, execute a strategy and manage risk — then test and refine it in a paper-trading account powered by Moomoo, using the Moomoo MCP. No live capital is required. The top 10 entries are shortlisted for final judging.

This listing is for the in-person **Kickoff Workshop** on 16 November: a night of food, networking and AI trading insights. Bring your laptop.

**Agenda**

- 6:00 PM — Doors open, visit the Moomoo booth (light refreshments served)
- 7:00 PM — Opening address by Alibaba Cloud
- 7:10 PM — Presentation by Moomoo
- 7:20 PM — Introduction to Qoder and demo
- 7:50 PM — Trading strategies sharing and introduction to the Moomoo API by a Moomoo in-house specialist
- 8:45 PM — Q&A
- 9:00 PM — End of event

**Timeline**

- 16 Nov, 6–9 PM — Kickoff Workshop with Qoder + Moomoo (this event)
- 17–28 Nov — Development and trading window
- 30 Nov, 23:59 SGT — Presentation submission deadline
- 11 Dec — Top 10 finalists shortlisted

**Prizes** — Over S$8,500 worth of cash, trading vouchers and Qoder credits.

**What you get** — Every approved participant receives a free one-month Qoder Pro Plan subscription (worth US$20) plus 1,500 credits (worth US$20), a US$100,000 initial paper-account balance, and access to the Moomoo MCP for the competition's paper-trading account. Your Qoder activation is linked to the same email used for your Luma registration.

**Who can join** — Open to all backgrounds, from algo traders and developers to AI builders and anyone curious about AI-powered trading.

**Judging criteria** (subject to change) — Risk-adjusted return (25%), Qoder & Qoder Cloud Agent usage (30%), Strategy & risk design (30%), Reusability & documentation (15%).

This competition uses simulated funds. Paper-trading performance does not predict live returns. Terms and conditions apply.
