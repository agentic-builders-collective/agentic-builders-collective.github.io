For the first time, Singapore's AI communities are coming together under one roof — uniting founders, builders, researchers, investors, and innovators from different corners of the ecosystem. The theme: "New era. New rules. New possibilities." The event explores a new era of work where AI is redefining what individuals can achieve: one-person companies scaling like entire teams, and traditional career paths giving way to new possibilities.

The evening also features facilitated speed-date networking — a series of 5-minute conversations with founders, investors, operators, builders, and professionals — plus partner booths. Registration requires host approval, and capacity is limited.

- 5:00–5:30 PM: Registration & Goodie Bag Collection
- 5:30–6:00 PM: Opening Speech + Partners Opening
- 6:00–6:45 PM: Panel 1 — Hire a Human or an Agent? Building the Next-Generation Workforce
- 6:45–7:00 PM: Break and booth visits
- 7:00–7:45 PM: Panel 2 — From Nine-to-Five to Super Individual: Building a One-Person Unicorn
- 7:45 PM–Late: Closing, followed by Buffet & Networking
