Can a friend tell you laughed before you send “haha”? In ProxyChat, two people open a chat on separate phones. When you turn on camera expressions, your avatar smiles or blinks on your friend's screen as you type.

The camera measures facial movements on your device. The chat sends expression values, and the other phone animates the avatar between updates. One detail we had to get right was what happens when tracking drops for a moment: the face settles back towards neutral. We used Codex while developing and testing the interface. You can also choose an expression by hand with the camera off.

Try a straight-face challenge with a friend. When did you spot their smile — while they were typing, or after the message arrived? [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/proxychat-by-sw/id6828853705) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.proxychat.myapp&hl=en&gl=SG)
