One founder. Zero employees. QwenWork and Hashmeta AI are calling founders, solopreneurs and AI innovators to build a one-person company that runs, grows and scales — with QwenWork agents as your entire workforce. Turn the challenges of running a business alone into production-ready solutions.

This listing is for the in-person **Kickoff Workshop** on 17 November: a night of food, networking and brainstorming. Bring your laptop. The build runs online after the kickoff, and the top 10 teams pitch live to a panel of judges from Hashmeta AI, AvePoint, Digital China and Alibaba Cloud.

**Prizes** — Win up to S$8,000 cash.

**Timeline**

- 2 Nov — Registration opens
- 17 Nov — Hackathon Kickoff Workshop with QwenWork (this event)
- 23 Nov — Submission deadline (3-minute video demo)
- 30 Nov — Top 10 finalists contacted via email
- 11 Dec — Live pitch and prize presentation

**What you get** — Every approved participant receives a free 1-month QwenWork subscription.

**Who can join** — Open to all backgrounds. No developer experience required.

**Tracks**

- Track 1: Business Operations & Automation — build AI agents that handle research, branding, web deployment, promo videos and investor decks, turning a solo founder's workflow into a self-running operation.
- Track 2: AI Governance, Security & Decision-Making — build the guardrails that keep autonomous agents safe, compliant and trustworthy, so you can scale without the risks that cost you clients.

**Judging criteria** — Innovation (25%), Feasibility & Viability (25%), Use of QwenWork Platform (20%), Demo Presentation (15%), Website built with Hashmeta AI (15%).

Supported by partners including Hashmeta AI, AvePoint Singapore, Digital China, BIMC, Sebrina Fund Management and Hikari Automation Systems.
