[Tangent](https://tangent.org.sg/) is a not-for-profit incubator for unconventional entrepreneurs in Singapore. Its second cohort ends on Friday 25 September, and the teams are sharing what they built in their four weeks at Tangent and what comes next. Each startup gets six minutes.

Tangent is AI-informed. Some of its founders build AI products, some build products with AI, and some build companies designed to run more efficiently on AI workflows. You'll see all three in this cohort.

If you're building something and thinking about turning it into a company, this is a low-commitment way to see what Tangent teams work on and how far they get in a month. Tangent is looking for founders for future cohorts.

This is a virtual event on Google Meet. Drop in any time and stay as long as you like. **Sign up on the [Luma page](https://luma.com/fxbfszds) to get the link** (registration needs host approval).

## Teams

1. **Chatclaim** (Abel Tan): a WhatsApp-based receipt processor for casual worker expense claims that saves managers hours each week.
2. **Chonnect** (Charmaine Lau): an AI-enabled B2B matchmaking platform pairing Singapore SMEs with freelance creatives.
3. **Closer** (Bryan Ng, Flora Tiew and Jabez Tho): a web and mobile CRM that tracks sales conversations, scores deals, and tells sales reps where to spend their next hour.
4. **Flo** (Rae Tan and Jiaxin Lin): an AI chat-agent staff member that takes on routine customer-service queries for SMEs.
5. **Kayabread** (Kaden Leow and Boon How Low): a one-stop AI accountant for founders tracking their business finances.
6. **ChurchAIReady** (Ezekiel Hoe): a faith-specific CRM for the relationships between church leaders, mentors, disciples and non-believers.
7. **The Tray** (Tan Yee Jing): a redesigned surgical procedure tray for safer, more intuitive clinical workflows and less consumables waste.
8. **Vibealogue** (Harish Kanna): a mobile app that lets non-technical users modify and add features to other mobile apps they use.
9. **VillainsLoved** (Tyra Ng): a fashion label for new-generation women.
