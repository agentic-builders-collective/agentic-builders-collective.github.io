Session 1 of the Lifelong Learning Singapore Thought Leadership Series explores how people can build sustainable, agile careers as AI reshapes the workplace.

## Keynotes

- **being-human/SKILL.md** — Adeline Sim, Executive Director and Chief Corporate Officer at HRnetGroup, discusses why judgment, adaptability, curiosity, fast learning and unlearning, and relationships remain enduring career advantages.
- **Will AI Replace Humans?** — Mario Jacober, Regional Head of HR Ops & Tech (APAC) at Zurich Insurance, shares the “Human-AI Decoder” approach to redesigning tasks and building an integrated human-machine workforce.

## Panelists

- Gerard Koh, VP, People Experience and Culture, Singtel
- Chelvin Loh, Director, Careers & Skills Intelligence Group, SkillsFuture Singapore and Workforce Singapore
- Dr. Tommie Chen, Partnership & Market Development Director, Institute for Adult Learning Singapore
- Mario Jacober, Regional Head of HR Ops & Tech (APAC), Zurich Insurance
- Adrian Tan, author of *No More Bosses*
